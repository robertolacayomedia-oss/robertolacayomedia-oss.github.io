About

I can run the shoot, light it and frame it. Then I sit down and cut it to the beat.

I'm Roberto Lacayo, a media producer and video editor based in Brussels. I studied International Media & Entertainment Business at Thomas More in Mechelen (2023–2026).

I've written and directed spots for national TV at VTV Honduras, edited 90+ World Cup capsules for La Fiesta del Fútbol, filmed and cut club nights for Midnight Voodoo, made the invitations for GOZA in Brussels and an aftermovie for Gelatina at Couleur Café.
